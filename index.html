Wild Data

Show1 and Show2 are kept as real-world sanity checks.

The controlled benchmark answers whether FocusVSR preserves paired-reference fidelity. The wild clips answer a different question: when the input has camera motion, compression, stage lighting, and no clean HR reference, does selective refinement produce more useful local detail without obvious temporal artifacts?

Project Idea

Repair only where the video is uncertain.

Video super-resolution is not uniformly difficult across a frame. Large structural regions can be reconstructed reliably by a strong recurrent model, while specular highlights, text, water, screens, and fast motion often remain ambiguous. FocusVSR treats that ambiguity as a routing signal instead of applying the same refinement everywhere.

The current prototype keeps BasicVSR++ as the stable reconstruction path and adds a conservative refinement layer for high-frequency regions. The goal is to improve local perceptual detail without sacrificing the temporal stability and distortion metrics that make BasicVSR++ a strong baseline.

Method

Uncertainty-aware selective refinement pipeline

01

Low-resolution clip

Input frames are degraded to the target 4x VSR setting and processed as a temporal sequence.

02

BasicVSR++ backbone

Bidirectional propagation recovers a stable full-frame reconstruction and preserves global structure.

03

Focus map

Residual cues, local detail energy, and temporal disagreement highlight regions that are likely under-restored.

04

Selective refinement

The refinement branch is applied only to uncertain high-frequency areas, then blended back into the backbone output.

Reliable regions

Flat backgrounds, rigid edges, and well-aligned structures stay close to the BasicVSR++ output to protect PSNR, SSIM, and temporal smoothness.

Uncertain regions

Text, lighting, crowd texture, water spray, and screen content receive more aggressive local refinement where perceptual errors are easiest to notice.

Qualitative Results

Effect demonstrations from our experiments

Concert scene: bright screens and dense crowd texture

The stage frame contains saturated light, thin screen boundaries, and repeated audience details. These are exactly the regions where a focus map can prevent full-frame over-processing and concentrate refinement where ambiguity is visible.

Real mobile video degradation

Handheld footage introduces glare, haze, and compression artifacts. The page keeps this sample because it reflects the kind of practical video users actually want to enhance.

High-frequency motion texture

Water and spray are difficult for pixel-aligned losses because many plausible details can match the same LR observation. This motivates reporting perceptual and temporal metrics alongside PSNR and SSIM.

Datasets

Vimeo is fixed to train 0:900, val 900:950, test 950:1000, and smoke 950:958. The final controlled table evaluates 50 test clips with zero failures; the wild paired table evaluates 1_from_high, 3_from_high, show1_from_high, and show2_from_high with 80 sampled frames per sequence.

Baselines

BasicVSR++ is the anchor at 35.7354 PSNR, 0.9462 SSIM, 0.0864 LPIPS, and 55.5511 FID-50. Real-ESRGAN alone drops to 28.0246 PSNR and 129.5927 FID-50, while our trained CFM alone reaches 30.2399 PSNR and 149.9160 FID-50, so both are treated as detail candidates rather than full-frame replacements.

Metrics

PSNR/SSIM/LPIPS are reported on the paired center frames. FID is reported only as small-sample FID-50 for Vimeo and FID-80 for wild paired clips. A simple exported-video temporal sanity check gives show1 mean Y frame diff 34.8129 for BasicVSR++ versus 34.8369 for FocusVSR-HQ, and show2 36.7524 versus 36.8018.

Ablations

H tests raw uncertainty fusion and reaches 34.8054 PSNR with CFM or 34.2038 with Real-ESRGAN. HQ recovers visual detail at 35.3201 PSNR and 55.1194 FID-50. L reaches 35.7650 with CFM and 35.7726 with Real-ESRGAN. MS is the final controlled variant at 35.7725 PSNR, 0.9460 SSIM, and 54.8450 FID-50.

Current signal

BasicVSR++ remains the strongest full-frame reconstruction anchor. FocusVSR-MS is the metric-safe controlled-table path, while FocusVSR-HQ is the presentation path for sharper local detail under an uncertainty mask.

FID note

Our FID is computed between paired SR outputs and GT frames using only 50 Vimeo frames or 80 wild frames. Because the sample count is tiny relative to Inception feature dimension, the value is useful for within-table ranking, not for comparison to large-scale 50k-image generative FID scores.

Current Takeaway

A conservative layer, not a replacement for the backbone.

The final claim is intentionally conservative: BasicVSR++ remains the reliable global reconstruction path, while FocusVSR improves how refinement is routed. The best presentation variant keeps the backbone's low-frequency structure and selectively transfers high-frequency detail only where the focus map indicates uncertainty.
